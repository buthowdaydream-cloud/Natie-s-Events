About Natie's Events

Discover the story behind Zimbabwe's premier event planning company and meet the passionate team dedicated to creating unforgettable moments.

Our Story

Founded by Natalie Danga, Natie's Events began as a small passion project driven by a love for creating memorable celebrations. What started with just wedding planning services has blossomed into Zimbabwe's most trusted full-service event planning company.

Based in the heart of Harare, we've successfully executed over 500+ events ranging from intimate family gatherings to grand corporate galas. Our commitment to excellence, attention to detail, and personalized approach have made us the go-to choice for discerning clients across the country.

We believe that every event, regardless of size, deserves the same level of care, creativity, and professionalism. Our team combines local expertise with international standards to deliver experiences that exceed expectations.

Our Mission & Vision

Guiding principles that drive everything we do at Natie's Events.

Our Mission

To transform ordinary moments into extraordinary memories by providing exceptional event planning services that reflect our clients' unique visions and exceed their expectations.

We are committed to delivering personalized, stress-free event experiences through innovative solutions, meticulous attention to detail, and genuine care for our clients' satisfaction.

Our Vision

To be Zimbabwe's most trusted and innovative event planning company, recognized for setting industry standards in creativity, service excellence, and client satisfaction.

We aim to expand our reach across Southern Africa while maintaining the personal touch and quality that has made us successful, becoming the premier choice for unforgettable celebrations.
